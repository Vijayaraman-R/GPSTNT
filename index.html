    Frequently Asked Questions

    GPS Tours & Travels’ FAQ provides strict, professional guidance, addressing genuine client queries while deterring frivolous, unserious, or fraudulent inquiries.

    1. What services do you provide?

    We offer fully customized travel planning, international and domestic tour packages, concierge services, flight and hotel bookings, visa support, and emergency travel assistance. All services are handled professionally and with precision.

    2. How do I book your services?

    Bookings are accepted only through official channels: our website, email, or direct consultation with a GPS Tours & Travels representative.

    3. Are your tours customizable?

    Yes. Every itinerary is individually crafted based on verified client requirements. We do not offer generic packages or off-the-shelf solutions.

    4. Do you provide international travel assistance?

    Yes. This includes flights, hotels, visas, local transport, insurance, and 24/7 emergency support for verified travelers.

    5. Can you accommodate last-minute requests?

    We handle urgent requests only for confirmed clients with complete documentation. Unverified or speculative inquiries will not be entertained.

    6. Do you offer travel insurance?

    Yes. Guidance and facilitation of legitimate travel insurance is available for serious clients only.

    7. What is your cancellation and refund policy?

    Changes, cancellations, or refunds are strictly according to service providers’ policies and require official communication through our verified channels.

    8. How can I contact GPS Tours & Travels?

    Contact us via official phone lines, verified email, or scheduled appointments only. Messages from unverified sources may be ignored.

    9. Do you handle group or corporate travel?

    Yes, but only for pre-verified groups and corporate entities. All arrangements are professional and legally compliant.

    10. Why should I choose GPS Tours & Travels?

    Because we deliver professional, reliable, and fully accountable travel solutions. We prioritize serious clients and do not entertain casual or speculative inquiries.
